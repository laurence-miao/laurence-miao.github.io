## The work

At Amazon, I owned programs evaluating AI/ML service performance across video defect detection, automatic speech recognition, optical character recognition, and large language models. The work connected technical evaluation with recommendations for service teams.

## My contribution

- **Evaluation design:** gathered requirements across teams, designed technical methodologies, and coordinated reviews with subject matter experts.
- **Engineering and analysis:** worked on cloud infrastructure architecture, hands-on data analytics, and engineer coaching.
- **Program delivery:** developed roadmaps, aligned milestones with business owners, managed dependencies, and communicated progress to stakeholders.
- **Operational improvement:** identified opportunities to improve evaluation operations, tooling, and the software development lifecycle.

## Connecting evidence to action

The program outputs included evaluation methodologies, data analysis, product improvement recommendations, and delivery plans. These brought engineering findings and stakeholder decisions into the same process.
