## Research question

Can synthetic household financial narratives support assessments consistent with their source records, without requiring human-written reference narratives?

## The framework

1. **Establish fixed references.** A two-tier LLM jury derives assessments from structured household records. These references remain fixed when generation conditions change.
2. **Assess narratives independently.** The same jury evaluates each generated narrative under shared criteria, without seeing its source record or reference assessment.
3. **Compare the assessments.** Measure agreement with the references, variation across household groups, and how often jury responses are usable for comparison.

## Reported result

The public abstract reports a **32% reduction in the average gap between narrative-derived and reference financial-health scores** for a candidate condition relative to the baseline. The conditions differed in both prompt instructions and thinking mode.
