These are my favorite collections of AI agent skills. Each turns guidance into reusable workflows that an agent can follow for a specific task.

- **[mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)**: Matt Pocock’s engineering skills for clarifying requirements, planning changes, testing, debugging, and improving architecture.
- **[Research-Paper-Writing-Skills](https://github.com/Master-cai/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills)**: section-by-section guidance for writing machine learning, computer vision, and natural language processing papers, curated by Master-cai from Prof. Peng Sida’s open research notes.
- **[academic-research-skills](https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills)**: a Claude Code suite covering the full academic research pipeline, from literature review and writing to peer review and revision, with the researcher confirming each stage.
